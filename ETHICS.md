# Consent, Licences & AI Disclosure

* **Consent:** Any real person whose likeness, voice, or personal story is depicted must have documented informed consent recorded here before generation. Fictionalized/composite family storytelling avoids unauthorized likenesses.
* **Licences:** Pipeline code is provided for educational/challenge use; verify specific model licences (e.g., Apache 2.0 / MIT / provider terms) for commercial distribution.
* **AI Labelling & Provenance:** Every exported video includes a visible end card stating AI generation (`Made with AI`), and creators should enable platform AI disclosure labels upon publishing.
